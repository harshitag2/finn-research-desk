# Four-minute FINN demo

Use the final deployed build on a phone-sized viewport. Verify source access, model availability and the chosen filings immediately before presenting. The sequence is a rehearsal plan, not a claim that each new follow-on feature has already passed production checks.

## 0:00–0:40 — Start with the investor's inbox

Open the portfolio filing digest and choose a current time window. Show a real filing, its timestamp and source. Explain why it is relevant to a saved thesis or configured interest, then open the original disclosure. Routine filings remain available but do not dominate the screen.

If no material event occurred in the selected window, say so. Choose a broader period or an explicitly dated historical example; do not fabricate “new” news to make the demo exciting.

## 0:40–1:20 — Show the saved belief that can be wrong

Open a saved thesis with an explicit disconfirming condition. Follow the relevant item into the thesis review. Show the version of the thesis being reviewed, the source evidence and what deserves follow-up. Supporting and challenging retrieval routes are not themselves verdicts that a belief is true or false.

## 1:20–2:15 — Management promise versus delivery

Use Infosys FY26 as the source-checked example: original growth guidance 0–3%, later dated revisions, final 3–3.5% range and reported 3.1% constant-currency growth. Open both the promise and outcome pages. Explain that comparisons require the same fiscal period, currency basis and scope. Then show another sourced issuer in the generalized workflow, or explicitly demonstrate a no-guidance state rather than inventing a management target.

## 2:15–2:50 — Inspect cash, not only the narrative

Open the source-linked cash/capital view on an applicable industrial/services company. Show the reporting year, currency/scale and derived formula. A bank should receive an appropriate financial-institution response instead of a forced industrial-company cash-conversion score. Only show figures verified in the final release.

## 2:50–3:30 — Try a company outside the bundled demo set

Ask to research an unfamiliar NSE ticker. Show discovery, issuer identity, disclosure dates and one-step ingestion of a primary document, then ask a source-backed question. If the source host blocks a download, demonstrate the explicit manual-file recovery. This is more credible than pretending every ticker has instant complete coverage.

## 3:30–4:00 — Show judgment and efficiency

Enter `Set price alert to 5% and volume alert to 3x and use compact layout` and show the saved changes. Explain that price-only requests avoid the research model, missing disclosure periods stop synthesis, and source-backed calculations run in code. Close with the evaluation record: mechanical tests are reproducible, semantic financial claims still require source review, and a known retrieval miss was found and documented.

## Optional management-change example

If the selected real filing names an incoming executive, open the source-linked role/background card. Show the official filing or biography and effective date. A resignation without a replacement should remain “replacement not disclosed”. Do not use an unrelated profile with a matching name.

## Preflight

- Reviewers can access both deployed app and repository.
- The chosen source documents and company identity match the demo.
- Monitoring status reflects the last successful background run, not merely a visible timer.
- The build is current, and any evidence-only/provider-failure state is explained honestly.
- The original AI build-history export is attached separately; the decision summary is not represented as a transcript.
