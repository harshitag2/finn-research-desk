# Public ENAM research and product decisions

Reviewed on October 31, 2026. These findings inform an assignment prototype. FINN is not an official ENAM product, and no private ENAM systems were accessed.

## What could be verified

**ENAM Holdings' investment approach.** Its official philosophy discusses long holding periods, preserving capital, studying individual companies, management's allocation of capital, and independent judgment. Its About page adds management quality and governance; its Culture page emphasizes objectivity and questioning hypotheses.

Sources:

- [Investment philosophy](https://www.enam.com/investment-philosophy.html)
- [About ENAM Holdings](https://www.enam.com/about-us.html)
- [Culture](https://www.enam.com/culture.html)

**An adjacent ENAM-backed product: MProfit.** MProfit's official About page lists ENAM among its backers. Co-founder Manish Jain explicitly identifies Enam Holdings in his account of its January 2023 financing. MProfit's family-office product describes consolidating investments across entities and advisors and analyzing portfolio exposure.

Sources:

- [MProfit About](https://mprofit.in/about/)
- [MProfit co-founder's account](https://celestri.org/about/)
- [MProfit Family Offices](https://mprofit.in/family-offices/)

This establishes an investment relationship and an adjacent product workflow. It does not establish that ENAM built MProfit internally, that FINN has a commercial relationship with MProfit, or that its APIs are available to this prototype.

## What could not be verified

The public pages and focused searches reviewed did not establish a named internal ENAM Holdings AI research platform comparable to the assignment. The supplied challenge remains the clearest direct evidence of the requested FINN direction. A similarly named company at enam.co and separately marketed products called Finn were not treated as ENAM Holdings projects without evidence of an association.

Search scope: the official ENAM website and publicly indexed searches for ENAM Holdings with AI, engineer, FINN, platform, investment research and technology projects; then primary MProfit and founder pages to check the adjacent investment relationship. This is a bounded public review, not proof that no private project exists.

## Incorporated in FINN

| Public signal | Product interpretation implemented |
| --- | --- |
| Bottom-up research and management execution | A persistent company thesis file, linked to the existing issuer-scoped research engine |
| Independent judgment and questioning hypotheses | A required disconfirming condition; separate thesis and counter-evidence retrieval routes |
| Management, governance and preserving capital | Five lenses: business quality, management/capital allocation, cash quality, downside resilience and governance |
| Learning over time | Dated reviews retain the exact thesis version, evidence, citations and research trail; stale reviews are marked after edits |
| MProfit's portfolio consolidation and exposure workflow | A small generic INR holdings CSV importer, account aggregation, company weights and sector exposure |

The five lenses and their exact prompts are FINN product decisions, not a claimed ENAM checklist. Retrieved passages are not automatically labelled as supporting or refuting a belief. With a configured model, optional stance labels are model interpretations and must carry valid retrieved citations.

The portfolio importer is browser-local. It does not connect to MProfit, parse its proprietary exports, calculate tax or XIRR, or claim reconciliation with a broker. Snapshot values and sector labels come from the CSV, not from invented or silently repriced data.

## Boundaries retained

- Company research remains grounded in annual reports and management transcripts. The public ENAM/MProfit research is design context and is not mixed into a company's financial evidence.
- No implied partnership, copied internal interface, ENAM logo or affiliation claims.
- No background monitoring or automatic notification claims. Review dates appear within the thesis file.
- Browser-local thesis history and holdings do not synchronize across devices.
- Model synthesis remains optional and unconfigured until the user connects a provider.
