# Research answer and timeline coverage repair

The reported TCS example was a source-backed statement presented with insufficient scope. The bundled Q4 FY26 transcript (9 April 2026, physical PDF page 3) explicitly says the company does not provide specific revenue or earnings guidance. This is evidence of the statement in that call, not a verified history of every FY26 quarter.

The research pipeline now marks year-long guidance-change requests partial when the relevant four quarterly transcripts are not indexed. It lists available/missing quarters per issuer; document count alone cannot establish temporal completeness. It supplies that coverage constraint to the model and retains a deterministic coverage warning after synthesis.

For a cited, first-person no-guidance policy in a transcript, the result shows the exact quote, issuer, date, reporting period and physical page. It removes the overbroad “no revenue guidance exists” gap, preserves the missing-period caveat, and offers explicit demand/deals/risks and reported-growth follow-up questions. Missing numbers or analyst questions alone never create this policy panel.

Suggestions follow the selected issuer and available quarters. A company with a single call receives a question about that indexed call, rather than a year-long changes prompt. The historical prompt is offered only for a year with four indexed quarterly calls. Ordinary research uses an answer drawn from cited findings instead of a generic success sentence. Supports/challenges labels are restricted to thesis reviews, including when rendering older saved results.

Regression tests include the actual TCS passage, incomplete vs complete FY26 coverage, dynamic suggestions, analyst-question exclusion, source-less cases, and removal of stance labels from ordinary model results. The change does not invent guidance or substitute unrelated-company targets.
