# FINN workspace brief

Rebuild FINN as a professional investment research workspace controlled through one natural-language command bar. An investor should be able to ask a question, compare companies, request prices, find disclosures, open a thesis, navigate, and save or export research without learning a tool menu.

Make the page respond to the investor’s intent: resolve companies and scope, display the action being executed, open the relevant canvas, and keep original evidence close. Combine quotes and document research when the request asks for both. Use a restrained, high-density visual system with clear hierarchy, a compact navigation rail, meaningful source-backed charts, an evidence map and responsive layouts. Every interactive chart point or research claim should lead to its source.

Treat company disclosures as the evidence base. Preserve dates, fiscal periods and physical PDF pages. Separate management statements, analyst questions and achieved results. Identify missing coverage before calling a model; retain the user’s question and show a practical route to add sources. Never fill source gaps with invented financial facts. Use Gemini on the server, validate bounded actions and citations, and preserve original passages when synthesis is unavailable.

Preserve portfolio import, price tracking, thesis and counter-evidence review, document import, saved notes and exports. Do not imply affiliation with ENAM or access to its private systems. Build and verify the experience on desktop and mobile, then publish to the existing private FINN site.
